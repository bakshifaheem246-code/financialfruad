About Our Project

SAFE WEB PRO is a professional cyber security awareness platform developed as a Service Learning Project by BSc IT students.

The purpose of this project is to educate people about financial frauds, online scams, phishing attacks, fake calls and suspicious links.

Every year millions of users lose their money because they unknowingly share OTPs, bank details and passwords with fraudsters.

Our platform helps users identify suspicious activity, analyze scam messages and improve cyber awareness through interactive features.

The website includes:

  • AI Fraud Prevention Assistant
  • Advanced Fraud Message Detection
  • Fraud Number Scanner
  • Cyber Security Quiz
  • Online Safety Guidance
  • Fraud Reporting Awareness

SAFE WEB PRO promotes safe digital practices and helps users stay secure online.
